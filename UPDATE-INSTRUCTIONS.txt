IslandX v1.2.2 patch
Changed files:
- app/build.gradle.kts
- app/src/main/java/com/islandx/app/service/IslandNotificationListener.kt

This patch refreshes Android active media sessions when notifications change. The capsule's music icon and animated bars appear when IslandX receives a valid media session. Android Notification Access must be enabled, and IslandX Settings > Music must be enabled. Some music apps do not expose all media controls.
